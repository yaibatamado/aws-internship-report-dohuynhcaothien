### Key Topics

**FCAJ Community Day** focused on AI, cloud, and practical product-building experiences. Main sessions included:

* **Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You** - how context affects AI output quality, AI usage mindset, and career direction.
* **Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Quick** - AI assistants, intelligent workflows, and natural-language dashboard/report creation.
* **From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation** - Amazon CloudFront for cost optimization, security, reliability, and performance.
* **36 hrs with LotusHacks - Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality** - product ideation, hackathon execution, and practical lessons learned.
* **Non-Determinism of Deterministic LLM Settings** - why deterministic-looking LLM settings can still produce different outputs.
* **Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System** - a multi-agent system case study for startup credit scoring.

### Reflection

After attending the event, I better understood that applying AI effectively depends not only on the model but also on context, input data, workflow design, and output control. The CloudFront session was also directly related to the SmartHospital P2TB project, where the frontend/web application can be distributed through CloudFront to improve performance, security, and user experience.

### Relevance To My Project

The event supported my project work in several ways:

* Apply context-aware thinking when designing an AI assistant or chatbot for the hospital system.
* Understand CloudFront's role in frontend deployment and content distribution.
* Gain additional perspective on multi-agent systems for future AI features.
* Recognize the importance of turning ideas into a demoable and testable product.
