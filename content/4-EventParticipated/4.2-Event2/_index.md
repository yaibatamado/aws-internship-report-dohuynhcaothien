### Key Topics

**FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week** introduced practical experiences from an agentic AI buildathon. The event focused on how participants showed up, built working solutions, pitched ideas, and learned from the product-building process.

Main topics included:

* Agentic AI project ideation and buildathon execution.
* Team collaboration during a short product-building timeline.
* Pitching technical ideas clearly to an audience.
* Practical lessons from turning an AI idea into a demoable solution.
* Emotional and technical challenges during the build process, from uncertainty to flow and product confidence.

### Reflection

This event helped me understand that building an AI product requires more than choosing a model or writing prompts. A team needs to define a clear problem, break work into small deliverables, validate the user flow, and prepare a demo that can communicate value quickly. The sharing sessions also helped me see how technical confidence grows through iteration, feedback, and hands-on implementation.

### Relevance To My Project

The event is relevant to my MedChain AI / SmartHospital project because the project also includes AI-oriented features and a practical demo scope. The lessons from the buildathon can be applied to:

* Improve how the hospital AI assistant or medical summary feature is scoped.
* Present the project workflow more clearly during demo and evaluation.
* Focus on user-facing outcomes instead of only listing technologies.
* Plan future AI features with stronger attention to collaboration, testing, and product value.
